✦ FAQs

What to Expect

Everything you need to know before stepping into a hangout.

Is there video or voice chat? +

No. Sidekick is 100% text-based. There are no webcams, no microphones, and no video calls. It is designed for low-pressure, quiet co-presence without screen anxiety.

What do we actually do together? +

Once paired, you and your partner choose a virtual destination: wander through rain-lit Tokyo backstreets on 360° Street View, play casual web games like Skribbl.io or Lichess chess, or listen to an ambient lofi stream side-by-side with real-time text chat.

Is it safe? What if someone is rude or weird? +

Safety is built into every screen. A prominent Report / Block button is always visible in your chat header. One click terminates the session immediately, blocks that person permanently from your future queues, and logs the report. In addition, chat messages are pre-filtered by automated safety rules for slurs and solicitation.

Do I need an account, email, or payment? +

None. Sidekick is completely free, requires no email or login, and runs entirely in your web browser. Your temporary session data is discarded as soon as you close the hangout.
