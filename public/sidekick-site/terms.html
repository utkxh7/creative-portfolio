01 Community Safety Guidelines

Sidekick connects two strangers for a quiet, shared experience on the web. To keep this space thoughtful and safe for everyone, we enforce a zero-tolerance policy:

Absolute Prohibitions: No harassment, hate speech, slurs, sexual harassment or solicitation, or contacting/soliciting minors under any circumstances.
  • Strictly 18+: You must be 18 years of age or older to enter Sidekick. Minors are not permitted.
  • Instant Report & Block: An instant Report / Block button is always visible during your hangout. Clicking it terminates the session immediately, ensures you will never be matched with that user again, and logs the report for administrative review.
  • Automated Moderation: Real-time text chat is pre-filtered by automated safety rules. Messages containing slurs or explicit solicitation will be blocked at transmission.
  • Zero Tolerance for Abuse: Accounts or IP addresses associated with reported misconduct will be permanently barred from the matching queue.

02 Terms of Service

By using Sidekick, you agree to treat your fellow wanderers with respect and follow these simple terms:

  • Sidekick is provided on an "as-is" and "as-available" basis for personal, non-commercial entertainment.

  • We reserve the right to terminate or restrict access to any session, IP address, or user without prior notice if community guidelines are violated.
  • External destinations (such as Street View, games, or museum portals) are operated by third parties and subject to their respective terms.

03 Privacy Policy

Sidekick is intentionally designed with radical privacy and minimal data retention:

  • No Account Required: You do not need an email, phone number, or social login to use Sidekick.
  • Ephemeral Sessions: Handles, vibe selections, and chat messages are held only in temporary memory during active sessions and are discarded when a hangout ends.
  • No Third-Party Ad Trackers: We do not sell your personal data, track you across the web, or embed tracking pixels.
  • Safety Logs: When a user invokes the Report function, only the minimal incident payload (timestamp, reason, and temporary session identifiers) is logged solely for review and ban enforcement.
