Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

1. Who we are

Chingari ("we," "us," "our") operates chingari.co (or whichever domain you launch on) and sells collector's-edition art lighters. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit our site or place a pre-order, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

Registered entity: [Business/Proprietorship Name]. Contact for privacy questions: [support email].

2. What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, email, phone number, delivery address
  • Order details: which design(s) you pre-ordered, payment amount, edition number assigned
  • Payment information: we do not store your card, UPI, or bank details ourselves — these are processed directly by our payment partner (Razorpay), and we only receive confirmation that payment succeeded, not your full payment details
  • Basic usage data: pages visited, device/browser type, collected automatically for site performance and security

3. Why we collect it

  • To process and fulfil your pre-order, including contacting you about manufacturing timelines, balance payment, and shipping
  • To send order confirmations and (only if you opt in) updates about new drops
  • To respond to questions submitted through our contact form
  • To meet our own accounting, tax, and legal record-keeping obligations

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary data with: our payment gateway (Razorpay) to process payment, our manufacturing/fulfilment partner to ship your order, and any email/SMS/WhatsApp tool we use to send order updates — each acting as a data processor on our instructions, not as an independent owner of your data.

5. How long we keep it

We retain order and contact data for as long as needed to fulfil your order, resolve any disputes, and meet tax/accounting record-keeping requirements under Indian law, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to show you what personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time, by writing to [support email]. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. No online system is 100% secure, but we don't store payment card details ourselves, and access to customer data is limited to those who need it to fulfil your order.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the business grows. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: [support email]
