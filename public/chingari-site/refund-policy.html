Refund Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

1. Pre-order booking amount

Your pre-order booking amount (₹9) is fully refundable in the following cases:

  • We're unable to place a manufacturing run for your chosen design (e.g. it doesn't reach minimum order quantity) — you'll be notified and refunded automatically within 7–10 business days
  • You cancel your pre-order before the balance payment is collected — write to us at [support email] with your order details

2. Balance payment

Once manufacturing is confirmed and we collect the remaining balance from you, that amount becomes non-refundable except in the cases below, since it commits real manufacturing and shipping costs on our end.

3. Damaged or defective items

If your lighter arrives damaged or defective, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the issue at [support email]. We'll offer a replacement or full refund, our choice, once verified.

4. Wrong item received

If you receive a different design or item than what you ordered, let us know within 48 hours and we'll arrange a correction at no cost to you.

5. Change of mind

Because every piece is produced in small, numbered batches specifically for pre-order customers, we're unable to accept change-of-mind returns once the balance payment has been made and the order has entered production or shipped.

6. How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method via our payment partner (Razorpay), typically within 7–10 business days of approval, depending on your bank.

7. Contact

For any refund request: [support email]
