OUR STORY

Every Fire Needs
a Chingari.

Chingari means spark — the small thing that happens right before something bigger does. We started this studio around that one idea: that the object which lights everything else deserves to be more than an afterthought at the billing counter.

Most lighters are disposable by design — bought without thought, lost without notice. We wanted to make the opposite of that. Every Chingari starts as a full concept: a mood, a story, a reason to exist, worked out long before it ever touches steel. Then it's printed in small, numbered runs, refillable for as long as you keep it, so the object earns the place it takes up in your pocket.

We're a new studio, and we're building this in public. Chapter One is four designs, printed and numbered as a limited first run. Chapter Two is already taking shape — and we're actively looking to collaborate with independent illustrators who want their work carried in someone's pocket, not just on a wall.

If that sounds like you, or you just want to see what we make next — stick around. We're only getting started.

HOW WE WORK

Concept First,
Object Second

Every design goes through the same process: a concept, a mood board, dozens of revisions, before it's ever tested on a real lighter body. If it doesn't earn a story, it doesn't make the drop.

That's also why every piece is numbered. It's not a gimmick — it's a running count of exactly how limited each run actually is, so what you're holding is genuinely one of a few hundred, not one of a few million.

THE NAME

चिंगारी

Chingari is Hindi for "spark" — the small thing that starts something much bigger. We think a good lighter should carry that same idea: it's not just what starts the fire, it's what starts everything after.
